The Roundup
This month’s research is centered on EDC Blueprint Page, Accelerate Live Stream, Contextual Intelligence PDP. The strongest value is decision-shaping rather than broad launch approval: each track points to a clearer question about what users understand, trust, or know how to do next. The next cycle should resolve the remaining uncertainty around Platform Diagram Update.
Research Findings
01
EDC BLUEPRINT PAGE
Medium confidence

Users understand the Enterprise Data Cloud category, but the Blueprint page still needs to make the assessment and workshop outcome clearer before asking visitors to start. Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

One respondent asked directly: "Why would you assume I'd start an assessment straight away?"

DIRECTION

Clarify what the assessment produces, preview the value of the workshop or output, and validate whether visitors understand why starting the assessment is worth their time.

02
ACCELERATE LIVE STREAM
High confidence

The live stream experience needs to make the event feel live immediately and guide visitors toward one dominant “Watch Live” action. Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

Autoplay expectation mismatch creates friction for the keynote CTA

DIRECTION

Make “Watch Live” the dominant action, add immediate live-state cues, and test whether visitors can tell what is happening now versus what is available later.

03
CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE PDP
Medium confidence

Contextual Intelligence is recognizable as an AI and data management topic, but the differentiation and business value need to become clearer and more operational. Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

Most visitors grasp the category but miss the differentiation Responses clustered around generic labels, "AI," "data storage," "data transformation." Only after extended exposure did respondents use the product's own framing: "transforming fragmented data into AI-ready contextual intelligence."

DIRECTION

Translate contextual intelligence into concrete business value and operational trust cues, then validate whether users can explain what makes the capability different.

Meaningful Comparisons
01
PLATFORM DIAGRAM UPDATE
Medium confidence

Platform Diagram Update is best treated as a narrowed comparison problem: the next decision should protect the clearest user behavior rather than reopen broad creative exploration. Source deck ↗

CRITERIA

Choose the strongest direction based on first-glance comprehension, clarity of the next step, user confidence, and whether the page helps visitors complete the intended task.

DIRECTION

Simplify the platform diagram around one AI-ready data-platform story and test whether users describe the platform as intelligent and connected rather than only storage-oriented.

What Is Still Unresolved
01
Platform Diagram Update
Platform story and perception shift

Does the simplified diagram shift perception toward an intelligent AI-ready data platform, or does it still read like a storage architecture diagram?

02
EDC Blueprint Page
Assessment value and workshop clarity

Do visitors understand what they get from the assessment and why the workshop is worth engaging with before they have enough context?

03
Contextual Intelligence PDP
Differentiation and business value

Can users explain what contextual intelligence does differently and why that difference matters operationally?
