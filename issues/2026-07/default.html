The Roundup
This month’s research is centered on EDC Blueprint Page, Accelerate Live Stream, Contextual Intelligence PDP. The strongest value is decision-shaping rather than broad launch approval: each track points to a clearer question about what users understand, trust, or know how to do next.
Research Findings
01
EDC BLUEPRINT PAGE
Medium confidence

Users understand the Enterprise Data Cloud category, but the Blueprint page still needs to make the assessment and workshop outcome clearer before asking visitors to start.

Respondent quote: “Why would you assume I'd start an assessment straight away?” Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

In the 10-second first impression, most respondents called the page "data cloud storage" and fewer than 5 named the Success Blueprint unprompted; half clicked "Start Your Assessment," but expectations were inconsistent and 29% weren't sure it was for them.

DIRECTION

Clarify what the assessment produces, preview the value of the workshop or output, and validate whether visitors understand why starting the assessment is worth their time.

02
ACCELERATE LIVE STREAM
High confidence

Captured and implemented before launch: first-impression testing drove the deployed live-stream page — a dominant “Watch Live” action and immediate live-state cues, built on the final concept-testing variants. Post-launch, the on-site stream held engagement through both keynotes, peaking at 2,179 concurrent views on Day 1 (June 17) and 683 on Day 2 (June 18).

Respondent quote: “I am not sure if this promotes an event or if it enables me to view it on stream.” Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

Pre-launch: fragmented action intent (no dominant CTA), an autoplay mismatch (“Very unclear that this would autoplay”), emotional baseline only “ok.” Post-launch peaks are concurrent, incl. an est. ≥20% internal traffic; net viewership ~2,290 across both days, largely US/UK/Canada.

DIRECTION

Reuse the single-dominant-CTA and live-state pattern on future event pages, and instrument on-page “Watch Live” engagement — the stream was ungated, so attribute the page’s role in viewership rather than relying on sign-ups.

03
CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE PDP
Medium confidence

Contextual Intelligence is recognizable as an AI and data management topic, but the differentiation and business value need to become clearer and more operational. Source deck ↗

EVIDENCE

Most visitors grasp the category but miss the differentiation — responses clustered around generic labels ("AI," "data storage"). After a plain-language value statement was added, 66% understood it most or very well; "Watch a Demo" dominated in testing.

DIRECTION

Translate contextual intelligence into concrete business value and operational trust cues, then validate whether users can explain what makes the capability different.

What Is Still Unresolved

EDC Blueprint Page
Assessment value and workshop clarity

Do visitors understand what they get from the assessment and why the workshop is worth engaging with before they have enough context?

02
Contextual Intelligence PDP
Differentiation and business value

03
Accelerate Live Stream
Live-state orientation

How much of the sustained viewership is attributable to the page experience versus event promotion and the paid simulcast — and does the live-state, single-CTA pattern replicate on the next event page?
