Events page findings:
A more prominent “Explore All Events” button increased engagement
Higher contrast and clearer hierarchy improved orientation and first-click behavior.
Simpler layouts outperformed more visually complex/video-heavy directions.
Webinar landing page:
Needs to communicate value faster and make registration feel easier. “Exceeded Expectations” should be much higher. Quick recommendations:
Make it instantly clear this is a webinar, what users will learn, and how long it takes.
Rewrite headlines around attendee value instead of branded language.
Improve scanability, hierarchy, and contrast so users know where to focus immediately.
Make the form feel faster and easier to complete.
Reduce visual noise, decorative graphics, and distracting helper/disclaimer copy.
Clarify the CTA and what happens after clicking.
